O-

Universal Red Cell Donor

Type O Negative red blood cells possess neither A nor B antigens, and lack the Rh D antigen. Consequently, the recipient's immune antibodies will not recognize them as foreign, making O- the gold standard for immediate life-threatening trauma before blood grouping is known.

AB+

Universal Red Cell Recipient

Individuals with AB Positive blood have both A and B antigens on their red cells as well as the Rh factor. Because their bodies recognize all antigens as normal, their serum lacks anti-A, anti-B, and anti-Rh antibodies, enabling them to safely receive red blood cells from all eight types.
