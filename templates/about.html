Clinical Rule 90: Biological Recovery

Why 90 Days Between Donations?

When an adult donates 350-450ml of whole blood, the body replenishes fluid plasma volume within 24 to 48 hours. However, your bone marrow requires approximately 8 to 12 weeks to synthesize replacement red blood cells and restore iron ferritin stores to safe baseline levels.

24-48 Hours

Blood plasma fluid volume fully rehydrated

56-84 Days

RBCs & hemoglobin levels completely regenerated

12 Weeks

Recommended minimum interval for male & female donors

Eligible Donor Criteria

Standard criteria approved by National Blood Transfusion Council

  • ✓ Age: Between 18 and 65 years old.
  • ✓ Weight: Minimum 45 kg (whole blood 350ml) or 55 kg (450ml).
  • ✓ Hemoglobin: Not less than 12.5 g/dL measured at the blood bank.
  • ✓ Vitals: Normal pulse (60-100 bpm) and blood pressure.

Temporary Deferrals

Circumstances where you should postpone donation

  • ! Tattoos or Body Piercings: Defer for 6 months from the date of procedure.
  • ! Major Surgery: Defer for 6-12 months until full recovery.
  • ! Antibiotics / Infection: Defer for 7 days post completion of antibiotics.
  • ! Alcohol Consumption: Avoid alcohol for 24 hours prior to donation.
